# Steward's Grievance Checklist

> A practical, step-by-step checklist for handling a grievance from first
> conversation to resolution. Adapt steps and time limits to your own contract.
> Operational guidance, not legal advice.

## Phase 1 — Intake (the member comes to you)

- ☐ Listen fully before reacting. Let the member tell the whole story.
- ☐ Get the facts: **who, what, when, where, why, and who witnessed it.**
- ☐ Get key details in writing or take careful notes.
- ☐ Ask what outcome the member wants (the remedy).
- ☐ **Check timeliness immediately** — when did they become aware, and what is
  the filing deadline under the contract? This is where grievances are lost.
- ☐ Reassure the member; don't promise an outcome you can't guarantee.

## Phase 2 — Investigate

- ☐ Read the relevant contract article(s), and consider past practice, policy,
  and law.
- ☐ Gather documents: discipline notices, emails, schedules, pay records,
  handbook pages.
- ☐ Interview witnesses; get written statements where possible.
- ☐ **Request information from the employer** you're entitled to (relevant
  records) — in writing.
- ☐ Identify the theory of the violation: contract language, past practice,
  unequal treatment, or unlawful action.

## Phase 3 — Assess (is there a grievance?)

- ☐ Is it timely?
- ☐ Is there an actual contract violation, or just an unfair situation?
- ☐ How strong is the evidence? What's missing?
- ☐ Decide: **file**, keep investigating, refer to your labor rep, or explain to
  the member why it doesn't meet the standard.

## Phase 4 — File

- ☐ File at the **correct step** and **before the deadline.**
- ☐ Put it in writing: facts, article(s) violated, and the remedy sought.
- ☐ Keep the language factual — avoid personal attacks and speculation.
- ☐ Keep a **dated copy** of everything you submit and receive.

## Phase 5 — Represent

- ☐ Prepare the member for the meeting; agree on who says what.
- ☐ At the meeting, stay professional and stick to the facts and the contract.
- ☐ Take notes on management's response and reasoning.
- ☐ After each step, record the decision and the **next deadline.**

## Phase 6 — Track & escalate

- ☐ Log the grievance and its deadlines (use the Grievance Tracker).
- ☐ If denied and the case has merit, **advance to the next step on time.**
- ☐ Keep the member informed at every stage.
- ☐ When resolved, confirm the remedy is actually carried out.

## Do

- ☐ Act fast — deadlines are unforgiving.
- ☐ Document everything, contemporaneously.
- ☐ Keep the member informed and honest expectations.
- ☐ Treat management professionally; you'll deal with them again.

## Don't

- ☐ Don't miss a deadline — file to preserve the case even if you're still
  investigating.
- ☐ Don't promise to "win."
- ☐ Don't argue the merits in the hallway; use the process.
- ☐ Don't go it alone on a hard case — use your labor rep.

---

_Adapt steps, time limits, and article references to your current collective
bargaining agreement. Not legal advice._
