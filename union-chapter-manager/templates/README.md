# Union Steward Templates

Standalone, sellable template assets. They work on their own (list them on
Etsy / Gumroad today) **and** double as the top-of-funnel lead magnets for the
course and the Union Chapter Manager app — the same audience buys all three.

Each template ships as a **Markdown source** (version-tracked, easy to edit) and
a **formatted file** ready to download and rebrand.

| Template | Files | What it's for |
|----------|-------|---------------|
| **Grievance Intake Form** | `.md` · `.docx` | Capture one grievance in depth — parties, articles, narrative, remedy, deadlines, signatures, step log |
| **Grievance Tracker** | `.xlsx` | A running log of *all* grievances with automatic deadline alerts and a dashboard |
| **Weingarten Rights One-Pager** | `.md` · `.docx` | Member-facing guide to the right to representation in investigatory meetings |
| **Steward's Grievance Checklist** | `.md` · `.docx` | Step-by-step handling checklist, intake to resolution |

## Suggested product: "Union Grievance Toolkit"

Bundle all four as a single listing. Suggested price **$19–29** — well above a
single template, cheap enough to be an easy yes, and it captures the email you
then market the course and app to.

## Positioning note

Most grievance templates for sale are **generic HR / management-side**. These are
**union-steward-specific** (CBA articles, step deadlines, Weingarten, member
remedy) — a genuinely underserved angle and the same wedge that runs through the
course and the app.

## Important

These are operational tools, **not legal advice**. Every template tells the buyer
to verify time limits, steps, and article references against their own collective
bargaining agreement. Keep that disclaimer in place.
